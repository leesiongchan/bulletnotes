Connect your
SilverBullet space.

Start with local scratchpads. Connect your own SilverBullet server when you want to bring them into your existing notes.

Add your server address and token in Settings. Your scratchpads sync to Quick Notes/ when the server is available, and you can search and open pages from your space.
